Research

I study complex fluids and flows in porous media with simulations, experiments, and scientific machine learning. I build simplified model problems that keep the essential physics of industrial and environmental systems, from mixing in yield-stress fluids to contaminant removal underground and radionuclide release from nuclear fuel.

I am a Research Scientist at McMaster University in the Zhao Lab, with Dr. Robin Zhao. I did my PhD at Concordia University with Dr. Ida Karimfazli.

Yield-Stress Fluid Mixing: Localisation Mechanisms and Regime Transitions

See our paper in the Journal of Fluid Mechanics on how a yield stress confines mixing around a moving stirrer. As the yield stress grows (left to right), shed vortices first escape, then stay trapped near the stirrer, and finally stop forming. More

Impact Dynamics of Polymeric Hollow Droplets

See our paper in Physics of Fluids on hollow water and polymer droplets hitting a surface. Adding polymer weakens the central counter-jet, delays bubble rupture, and keeps the droplet from detaching. More
